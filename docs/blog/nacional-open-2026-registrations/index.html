Nacional Open 2026: 1,007 athletes, 100 teams and a Gracie Barra wall

We grouped every branch on the IBJJF list. One team brings more athletes than the next two combined.

Oct 22, 2026·bjj.stat
1,007
registrations, 148 team entries, 100 teams once branches are grouped.

More than #2 and #3 combined

Gracie Barra’s 178 registrations beat Brazilian Power Team and Focus together (144). Its five biggest branches are Montijo (27), Cascais (26), Paço de Arcos (25), Belas (24) and Carnaxide e Queijas (18). Counted one entry at a time, the biggest single school is Brazilian Power Team with 69, followed by Focus Jiu-Jitsu with 66.

Who’s on the mats

  • 1 in 4 registrations are women (256).
  • White belts outnumber black belts about 3 to 1 (254 vs 85).
  • 216 of 455 divisions have only one athlete so far. The busiest is white belt, adult male, feather, with 15.
Registrations are still open, so these numbers will move. In 2025 only 14% of divisions ended with a single athlete.
