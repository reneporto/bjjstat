Lisbon Open 2026

The perfect weekend: three athletes won every gold they could

Weight and open class, gi and no-gi. 4 golds out of 4, and all three compete in Master 3.

Oct 22, 2026·bjj.stat
4/4
golds for three athletes at the Lisbon Open 2026.

Of the 559 athletes who reached a podium, 25 took home four medals and only 3 made all four of them gold: Rebeca Cifrian Montoya (VA Jiu-Jitsu Luxembourg), Leigh Snowden (Lucio Sergio BJJ Lifestyle) and Anca Cublesan (Brazilian Power Team International).

Three different teams, two brown belts and one black belt, 12 golds between them. Every perfect weekend came from the Master 3 (41+) division.
