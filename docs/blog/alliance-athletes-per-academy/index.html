54 athletes, one academy: Alliance leads the Nacional Open per location

Gracie Barra brings the most athletes. But divide by academies and Alliance’s single Lisbon gym comes out on top.

Oct 22, 2026·bjj.stat
54
registrations from a single Alliance academy in Lisbon.

The IBJJF list for the Nacional Open Portugal 2026 shows Alliance under five names (Parque das Nações, SP, Portugal, International, Porto Alegre). They all train at the same Lisbon academy, so we count them as one. That makes Alliance the 4th team by registrations and the #1 team per academy.

From outside the top 10 to #4

At the 2025 Nacional Open, Alliance won 16 medals (6 gold) and did not make the team top 10. One year later, only Gracie Barra, Brazilian Power Team and Focus have more athletes registered.

2025 is measured in medals and 2026 in registrations, so this is a signal of growth, not a like-for-like result. We will compare medals after Nov 7–8.

Adults, masters and new blood

Alliance is lighter on kids: 26% of its registrations, against 37% across the event.
